# Wattwise delivery outcomes

## Interactive appliance dashboard — complete
- Show at least five clearly labeled appliances, each with a wattage rating and an on/off control.
- Recalculate estimated energy use and the running electricity bill immediately whenever an appliance state changes.
- Show a visible target bill with a clear under/near/over comparison.
- Explain which active appliances contribute most to energy use and cost.

## Power-cut challenge — complete
- Provide a Power cut control that triggers a clearly differentiated simulated outage mode.
- Require exactly three appliances to remain running during the outage; active appliances are capped at exactly three.
- Provide comfort-versus-cost feedback for the selected trio.

## Presentation-ready UX — complete
- Use responsive layout, clear status messaging, polished interactions, and no smart-home IoT dependencies.
